Outreach Programs

Taking quality care to the community

Good health shouldn’t depend on where you live. Our outreach programs bring screenings, education and essential supplies directly to underserved communities, and we partner with organisations that share that mission.

  • Free health screenings (blood pressure, blood sugar, BMI and more)
  • Health education and awareness campaigns
  • Donation and distribution of medical supplies
  • Partnerships with NGOs, churches, schools and corporate CSR teams
